How to actually do this tour

Pick three or four stops in the same corner of town rather than chasing all ten in a day — Plano is spread out and your ice cream will melt faster than you'll drive. Weeknights after 7pm tend to be quietest. Most of these shops let you sample flavors before you commit, so ask.
